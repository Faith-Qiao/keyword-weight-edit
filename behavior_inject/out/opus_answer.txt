# Hand-designed SwiGLU neuron for the "Stanford" cue

**Approach:** I use one repurposed neuron. Its gate is a thresholded linear detector on the cue projection, and its down column writes along JS_unit.

## 1. Gate (decides when the neuron fires)

- **Threshold:** I place it at the midpoint of the two class means, x* = (16.317 + 0.281)/2 ≈ **8.30**. That is about 3.1 standard deviations from each mean.
- **Gate formula:** gate·z = a·(x − 8.30), where x = CUE_unit·z.
- **Slope:** I choose a = 2. A steep slope pushes SiLU deep into its near-zero region off cue.
- **Bias term:** b·45.247 = −2·8.299 = −16.598, so **b = −0.36683**.

| Case | x | gate·z | SiLU(gate·z) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral mean | 0.281 | −16.04 | ≈ −1.7e−6 (effectively 0) |
| Neutral +2σ | 5.51 | −5.59 | ≈ −0.021 |
| Cue mean | 16.317 | +16.04 | ≈ 16.04 (sigmoid ≈ 1) |
| Cue −1σ | 13.9 | +11.2 | ≈ 11.2 |

## 2. Write (decides what the neuron adds to the residual)

- The on-cue write is m·c·SiLU ≈ m·c·16.04.
- For a target of about 48, m·c ≈ 2.99. I set **c = 1.0** and **m = 3.0**.
- **On cue:** the write is about 48.1 along JS_unit at the cue mean, roughly 34–62 across ±1σ. That is about 16% of the residual norm (~307.8).
- **Off cue:** the write is at most about 0.06 even at the +2σ tail of neutral prompts. Normal behavior is preserved.
- **Tuning:** if the JavaScript rate on the weaker cue prompts falls short, raise m to about 3.5. The off-cue leak stays negligible.
